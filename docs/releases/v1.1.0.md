# 星云巡航 V1.1 / Nebula Cruise V1.1

## 中文

V1.1 将《星云巡航》升级为更完整的星际指挥中心体验：更灵敏的连续变道、原创 144 BPM 三轨自适应配乐、中英双语、本机 Top 15，以及按 `P` 暂停或继续。

### 本版亮点

- **轨道防线场景：** 12 张具备水平与俯视角变化的立体图集和 1 张道路边缘图集，让建筑在中央及边缘车道随距离呈现不同侧面和顶部。矮、中、高建筑分别使用 600、1,250、2,000 世界单位高度，并以 1 条青色灯带、2 条灯带和金色信标提示一段跳、二段跳及超级形态三段跳路线。
- **语义画面与事件视界：** Semantic Spectrum 语义配色将金色玩家飞船、石墨橙色建筑、深红敌对单位和冷黑缺口分离；连通缺口会组成一个危险事件视界，同时保留精确路面边界和警示灯。
- **连排与重型蜂群机：** 发光矮墙和中墙可以组成连续防御走廊；Scout / Striker 重型蜂群机使用新的石墨深红轮廓，同时保留落点、预警、移动方向、碰撞与换道逻辑。每条新增的进阶建筑或连排挑战都保留普通绕行车道。
- **推进反馈：** 二段跳、超级形态三段跳、持续滑翔和 BOOST 使用不同的双引擎矢量喷流；滑翔与 BOOST 还带有同步的低频轰鸣和中频喷气声，状态重叠时平滑切换且不会重复叠加。
- **操作与暂停：** 按住 `A` / `D` 或 `←` / `→` 连续移动；使用 `K` / `Space` / `W` / `↑` 跳跃，下落时按住即可滑翔；`J` 射击或蓄力；`Space` / `Enter` 开始或再来一局；`P` 暂停或继续；`M` 切换全部声音；任务结束后可按 `Esc` 返回指挥中心。
- **语言与本机记录：** 中文系统首次使用中文，其他系统首次使用英文，也可以手动切换并记住选择。本机 Top 15 和当前玩家名只保存在当前浏览器中；名字会自动沿用，也可以随时修改，且不会跨设备同步。

在线游玩目标：https://stanatny.github.io/skyroads/

通用 macOS 发布资产目标：`Nebula-Cruise-macOS-v1.1.0.zip`

V1.1 使用仓库内自托管并记录许可来源的视觉素材，保留 Apple Silicon 与 Intel 通用 WKWebView 包装目标，并将 `.agent/skills/ship-browser-games` 作为版本化 Skill 落库，供后续游戏设计、素材、测试和发布工作复用。

## English

V1.1 expands Nebula Cruise into a fuller interstellar command-center experience with more responsive held lane movement, an original adaptive three-stem score at 144 BPM, English/Chinese localization, a browser-local Top 15, and `P` to pause or resume.

### Highlights

- **Orbital Defense world:** Twelve upright yaw-and-pitch atlases plus one road-edge atlas give center and edge-lane structures different side and top views as distance changes. Low, medium, and high structures use 600, 1,250, and 2,000 world-unit heights, with one cyan band, two bands, and a gold beacon signaling one jump, two jumps, and the super-form third jump.
- **Semantic scene and event horizon:** Semantic Spectrum colors separate the gold player ship, graphite/orange structures, crimson hostile units, and cold-black gaps. Connected missing-road cells form one dangerous event horizon while preserving exact road boundaries and warning lights.
- **Corridors and Heavy Swarm drones:** Lit low and medium barriers can form connected defense corridors. Scout and Striker Heavy Swarm drones use new graphite/crimson silhouettes while retaining landing markers, warnings, movement direction, collision, and lane-change logic. Every newly added advanced building or corridor challenge retains an ordinary bypass lane.
- **Propulsion feedback:** Second jump, super-form third jump, sustained glide, and BOOST use distinct twin-engine vector jets. Glide and BOOST also add synchronized low rumble and mid-frequency fire/air noise, with smooth transitions instead of stacked loops.
- **Controls and pause:** Hold `A` / `D` or `←` / `→` to move continuously; use `K` / `Space` / `W` / `↑` to jump and hold while descending to glide; use `J` to fire or charge; `Space` / `Enter` starts or restarts a mission; `P` pauses or resumes; `M` toggles all audio; and `Esc` returns to the command center after a mission.
- **Language and local records:** Chinese systems start in Chinese and other systems start in English, with a remembered manual switch. The local Top 15 and current pilot name stay in this browser; the name is reused automatically, remains optional to change, and the data do not sync across devices.
