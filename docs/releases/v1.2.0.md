# 星云巡航 V1.2.0 / Nebula Cruise V1.2.0

## 中文

V1.2.0 带来蓄力式主动超级加速、教学模式与界面重设计：

- **燃料爆发**：燃料 ≥75% 且在地面上时，**按住 `W` 或 `↑` 蓄力 1 秒**触发主动超级加速 —— 消耗 50% 燃料，速度锁定 36 米/秒，持续 3 秒，期间无敌穿透。`W`/`↑` 专用于蓄力，不再承担跳跃（条件不满足时静默忽略，不会误跳）；1 秒内松手仅取消蓄力。跳跃只有 `Space` / `K`。爆发最后 1 秒有高亮金色边缘光晕 + 三声渐高预警；**结束瞬间**有金白爆闪、冲击波环、下行熄火音，并进入 **1 秒金色护盾无敌保护**，防止速度骤降瞬间撞障。燃料条就绪时有金色脉冲边框，蓄力时下方显示金色进度条。
- **新手教学模式**：指挥中心新增"新手教学"按钮，随时进入完整教学 —— 变道、跳跃、滑翔、射击、燃料爆发五个阶段，带"教学 n/5"进度标签。首次任务也会自动引导一次。**教学是练习场而非真实环境**：引导期间无敌（撞墙/掉坑/撞敌机不致死）、燃料锁定满格（最后一阶段的燃料爆发随时可练）、限速 16 段/秒。修复了教学在第一阶段后提前消失的问题（原先按 24 段热身区位置截断，约 3 秒就结束了；改为 90 秒时间保底）。只有真正完成五个阶段才标记"已看过"——超时或退出都不算，下次任务仍会自动引导，死亡后无需刷新页面即可重进。
- **界面重设计**：毛玻璃面板、青紫渐变标题、三段式蓝紫主按钮、胶囊工具按钮与版本徽章、金色蓄力进度反馈，整体更通透现代。主界面操作说明新增"燃料爆发"条目（中英文）。
- **稳定性修复**：修复加速（BOOST）进入到期预警窗口时画面永久冻结的问题 —— 渲染层一处重复条件块残留未定义变量，抛出异常后动画帧中断而音乐仍继续；已补回归测试覆盖三种到期预警（加速/燃料爆发/超级形态）。

## English

V1.2.0 adds a charged super boost, a training mode, and an interface redesign:

- **Fuel Burst:** with fuel at 75% or above on the ground, **hold `W` or `↑` for one second** to fire a super boost — it costs 50% fuel, locks speed at 36 m/s for 3 seconds with full invincibility. `W`/`↑` is now dedicated to charging and no longer jumps (it is silently ignored when conditions are unmet, so it can never misfire into a jump); releasing early simply cancels the charge. Jumping lives on `Space` / `K` only. The final second flashes a brighter gold edge vignette with three rising warning beeps, and **expiry** lands a gold-white flash, a shockwave ring, a power-down sweep, and a **one-second golden shield of invincibility** so the speed drop cannot throw you into an obstacle. The fuel bar pulses gold when ready and shows a gold charge bar while holding.
- **Training mode:** a new "Training" button in the command center starts the full five-phase tutorial anytime — lane change, jump, glide, fire, and Fuel Burst — with a "Training n/5" progress label. First missions also auto-guide once. **Training is a practice sandbox, not the real run:** while the guide is active you are invincible (walls, gaps, and enemies cannot kill you), fuel stays pinned to full (the Fuel Burst phase is always within reach), and speed is capped at 16 segments/s. Fixed the tutorial vanishing after the first phase (it was cut off at the 24-segment warmup line, reached in about three seconds; it now runs on a 90-second time budget). Only completing all five phases marks the tutorial "seen" — timing out or quitting does not, so the guide is offered again on later missions and is always reachable after dying, no page refresh needed.
- **Interface redesign:** frosted-glass panels, a cyan-to-violet gradient title, a three-stop accessible primary button, pill utility controls and version badge, and gold charge feedback throughout. The title-screen controls list now documents Fuel Burst in both languages.
- **Stability fix:** fixed a permanent screen freeze when a BOOST entered its expiry-warning window — a duplicated render-branch remnant referenced an undefined variable, throwing mid-frame so the animation loop died while music kept playing. A regression test now covers all three expiry warnings (boost / Fuel Burst / super form).
